# Godot Marketing (Indie Strategy & Management)

## Overview

Visibility on Steam comes mostly from Steam itself and from content creators; everything else feeds those two. Start early: validate the hook publicly before heavy production, then build an audience alongside development. Give the user options and trade-offs; budgets and comfort with social media differ.

## Positioning

- One-line hook: fantasy + twist ("a cozy medieval village builder where every house is a papercraft diorama").
- Comparable titles: 2–3 games your players already love; use them on the store page and in pitches.
- Tags and capsule art must say the genre instantly.

## Marketing Beats (plan on a calendar)

1. Announcement (store page live, trailer/GIF, creator outreach).
2. Steam Playtest or closed beta (feedback + early community).
3. Demo launch (a few weeks before Next Fest).
4. Steam Next Fest (2027 editions open February 22 and June 14 — verify dates).
5. Release date announcement.
6. Launch (Early Access or 1.0), then updates as new beats.

For each beat: contact creators about two weeks ahead, prepare short clips, a Steam news post, Discord announcement, press note.

## Content That Works For Builders

- Building timelapses, before/after of a settlement, satisfying placement moments, "day 1 vs day 100".
- Devlogs: honest problem → solution stories, design decisions, art style evolution. Audiences respond to the developer's process more than polished ads.
- Short-form vertical video for reach; long-form (YouTube devlogs, streams) for depth.
- Pick two platforms you can keep up with; consistency beats volume.

## Content Creators

- Build a list of creators who covered similar games (builders, cozy, strategy): YouTube, Twitch, TikTok. Small and mid-size creators (1K–50K) are often the most responsive.
- Personal, short emails with a key, a 1-line hook, a GIF, and what's new; no generic blasts.
- Make the game creator-friendly: streamer-safe music option, readable UI on stream, interesting first 10 minutes.
- Paid ads rarely pay off for small indie games; test small if at all.

## Community

- Discord with clear channels (announcements, feedback, bugs, screenshots); an in-game feedback button that links there or to a form.
- Share player creations (screenshots, villages); run small challenges.
- Steam Playtest: free; collect feedback with an in-game form; use it before the demo to fix onboarding.

## Store Conversion

- See `godot-steam-release` for the store page; track visits → wishlists conversion in Steamworks and iterate on capsule, first screenshots, and short description.

## Measuring

- Wishlists per beat, store page conversion, demo median playtime, Next Fest followers, Discord joins.
- Keep a simple marketing log (date, action, result) to learn what works for this game.
