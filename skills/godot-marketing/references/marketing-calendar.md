# 12-Month Marketing Calendar Template (adjust to the project)

| Month | Development | Marketing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vertical slice, art direction locked | Hook test: share GIFs on 2 platforms; collect reactions |
| 2 | Core loop solid | Steam page "Coming Soon"; announcement trailer/GIF; creator list started |
| 3 | First systems complete | Weekly devlog clips; Discord opens |
| 4 | Content expansion | Steam Playtest (small); in-game feedback form |
| 5 | Onboarding pass | Playtest results → fixes; devlog about changes |
| 6 | Demo build polished | Demo live a few weeks before Next Fest; creator outreach 2 weeks prior |
| 7 | Next Fest | Daily social posts, livestream, respond to feedback |
| 8 | Post-fest fixes | Summary devlog; release date decision |
| 9 | Content + polish | Release date announcement; trailer 2 |
| 10 | Release candidate | Press kit, creator keys, launch discount plan |
| 11 | Launch | Launch-day posts, creators, fast patches |
| 12 | Post-launch | Update roadmap, first content update, seasonal sale |

## Source notes (verify before quoting)
- Steam Next Fest 2027 editions reported to open February 22 and June 14, 2027 (Playio blog, Oct 2026).
- Steam Playtest is free for developers and players; Valve suggests developers collect feedback themselves, e.g. a feedback button in the main menu (Steamworks docs).
- Creator outreach about two weeks before each beat; short-form creators drive reach; streamers/reviewers often expensive for small indies (practitioner advice, 2026).
- Devlog / building-in-public content performs well with 2026 audiences experiencing ad fatigue (practitioner blogs, 2026).
