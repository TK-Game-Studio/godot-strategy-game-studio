# Steam Market Realities for 2026–2027 Releases

Compiled November 2026. Figures move fast; re-verify with a search before quoting them to the user.

## Next Fest is crowded
- October 2026 Next Fest had about 7,240 participating games, up ~65% from February 2026 and roughly 8x since June 2023; demos were about half of projected 2026 Steam releases. (How To Market A Game)
- Followers/wishlists before the fest correlated most with demo peak players; top performers had demos live ~19 days before the fest versus ~37 for the rest — launching a demo months early did not help. (How To Market A Game)
- Most games get very little: outside the top ~100 by peak players, the median follower gain in the final week was near zero. (How To Market A Game)
- One developer documented far fewer wishlists per impression in Feb 2026 than in Feb 2023 at a similar starting point. (Recognizing Patterns)
- Genres with strong demo engagement in Oct 2026 included RTS, Strategy RPG, Life Sim, and City Builder; platformers were weakest. (How To Market A Game)

## 2027 calendar
- Steam Next Fest 2027 editions reported to open February 22 and June 14, 2027 (Playio blog citing Steam, Oct 2026) — confirm on Steamworks.
- Steam Playtest is free and Valve's preferred way to run closed betas; Valve leaves feedback collection to developers (e.g. a feedback button in the main menu).

## Cozy is booming in supply, not guaranteed in sales
- ~1,041 Cozy-tagged games released on Steam Jan–Sep 2026, up 143% vs the same weeks of 2025; only about 11% reached 1,000 reviews; Steam added the tag in a May 2026 tag overhaul, which inflates part of the growth. (Steam Page Analyzer)
- Takeaway: "cozy" alone is not a hook. Pair it with a distinctive mechanic or visual identity.

## City builders / colony sims
- Strong 2026 1.0 launches: Timberborn (March 12, 2026, Overwhelmingly Positive, 40k+ reviews) and Going Medieval (1.0 March 17, 2026). (GameBrief; Wikipedia)
- Manor Lords (mostly one developer at first) sold over 2.5 million copies by end of 2024. (GamesRadar)
- Design direction: spatial, visible simulation and answers to late-game sprawl (see `godot-game-studio/references/genre-design-patterns.md`).

## AI disclosure
- Valve rewrote the disclosure in January 2026: efficiency tools (e.g. coding assistants) used behind the scenes are exempt; AI-generated content players consume (in-game or marketing) must be disclosed; live-generated content needs guardrails described. (PC Gamer; VGC; Notebookcheck)
- In Oct 2026 Next Fest, only 2 of the top 25 games disclosed AI use (8%) versus 35% of the rest (self-reported). (How To Market A Game)

## Steam Deck / Steam Machine
- One compatibility review covers both; Verified requires full controller access by default, automatic on-screen keyboard for text input, and readable text. Many city builders only get "Playable" because small tooltips fail readability at 800p. (Steamworks docs; Switchblade Gaming)

## Engine timeline (Godot)
- Godot 4.6 (January 2026): Jolt default for new 3D projects, D3D12 default on Windows, new editor theme.
- Godot 4.7 (June 18, 2026): HDR output, AreaLight3D, Asset Store overhaul; 4.7.2 maintenance in August 2026.
- Godot 4.8: beta 4 on November 18, 2026; stable expected late 2026 / early 2027 (.NET build requires .NET 10).
