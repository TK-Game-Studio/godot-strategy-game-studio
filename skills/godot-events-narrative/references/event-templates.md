# Event Templates

## Threat with preparation window
- Day N: warning ("Scouts report wolves gathering in the north forest.")
- Day N+3: event happens; outcome depends on preparation (walls, guards, stock).
- Aftermath: chronicle entry, mood change, optional follow-up.

## Dilemma
- Situation: "Refugees ask to join. Food is short."
- Options: accept (+population, −food, +hope) / accept only workers (+workers, −mood of some) / refuse (−hope, +safety).
- Follow-up 20 days later referencing the choice.

## Windfall with a catch
- "A merchant offers rare seeds at half price."
- Options: buy (−gold, unlock crop, small chance of blight event later) / decline.

## Character moment (colony sims)
- Trigger: two characters with high relationship + festival.
- Result: marriage, celebration, mood boost; chronicle entry.

## Law / edict
- Unlock condition, cost, immediate effect, ongoing effect, conflicting laws, society reaction, ending flag.

## Scenario goal set
- 3 primary goals (e.g. reach 100 people, complete monument stage 2, survive 3 winters)
- 2 optional challenges
- Ending text varies by flags gathered from laws and dilemmas
